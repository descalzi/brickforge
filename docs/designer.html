A testable step towards text-to-brick design.

The planner supports rectangular brick and plate bodies, explicit empty spaces, profiled window frames and matching extruded slopes. Specialist previews use pinned LDraw surfaces. Wheels, curved surfaces and arbitrary specialised assemblies remain challenge cases. A connected nominal graph does not prove strength, stability or physical clutch fit.
