The planner supports structural bodies, explicit empty spaces, selected frames and slopes, tyre/rim assemblies, wheel mounts, tapered wings and round elements. Specialist previews use pinned LDraw surfaces. Coverage is curated; arbitrary objects and unsupported joints can remain blocked. A connected nominal graph does not prove strength, stability or physical clutch fit.
