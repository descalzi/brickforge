# LDraw surface attribution

The triangle surfaces in this directory are derived from the official LDraw
parts library mirror at [kulits/ldraw-parts, pinned revision
c70360d1c588be894997b2168c61909bc8058c4d](https://github.com/kulits/ldraw-parts/tree/c70360d1c588be894997b2168c61909bc8058c4d/ldraw).

`sources.json` retains the author and license notice from every recursively
referenced source file. Each mesh also retains its top-level author, license
notice and source revision. Changes: recursive transforms were applied and
surfaces triangulated and rounded to six decimal places for browser display.
Connection profiles and silhouettes are separately authored nominal data.
