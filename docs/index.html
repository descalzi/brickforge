From a shape to a buildable brick model.

BrickForge is an engineering project to turn descriptions, images and 3D geometry into constructions made from real LEGO elements — then prove that the parts connect and the model can be built.

THE METHOD

The hard part isn't the prompt.
It's making the pieces connect.

01

Understand the shape

Interpret the request, inspect the geometry, and identify the surfaces, dimensions and details that matter.

INPUTMesh + design brief
02

Find the right elements

Search real part geometry and candidate placements. Combine volumetric and surface-aware strategies rather than forcing every object into a voxel grid.

SEARCHParts + transforms
03

Prove the construction

Check connection compatibility, collisions, connectivity, support and whether the build can be assembled in a sensible order.

VALIDATIONAssembly graph
04

Make it buildable by a person

Generate a consistent 3D model, parts inventory and illustrated steps from the same validated construction.

OUTPUTModel + BOM + steps

ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE

One construction.
Every deliverable.

The model, inventory and instructions must never drift apart. BrickForge is being designed around one canonical assembly graph so every output describes the same set of parts, positions and connections.
