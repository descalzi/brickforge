WHAT IS CHECKED

One model.
One source of truth.

The browser engine uses the same published connection profiles as the part explorer. It places the bricks, checks stud-to-receiver alignment, rejects overlapping nominal body envelopes and confirms that every part belongs to one connected assembly graph.

The preview uses nominal brick bodies and stud geometry. The downloaded LDraw model references the corresponding source part file, so a compatible local LDraw library is needed to open it in a desktop viewer.

This prototype constructs rectangular blocks using 2 × 4 and 1 × 2 part profiles. Collision checks use conservative body bounds; they are not detailed mesh collision checks and do not prove physical stability, clutch force or buildability under every real-world condition.
